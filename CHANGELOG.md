## 2026-10-22-review-1

Published component editions: RN-001 v1.3, RN-002 v1.2 and FS1 worked example v1.3. Paper 001, the general skill and the CSV reader retain their existing content in this release.

### Clarified using existing records

- Added C55 win counts, win rates and medians. The two development years sum to approximately -2.142R.
- Added post hoc concentration arithmetic for the frozen 2026 ledger: +12.886R originally, -0.677R after removing the largest winner, and -8.839R after removing the largest two. These are descriptive diagnostics, not new trade-selection rules or predeclared pass criteria.
- Identified the original Support/Mixed/Reject labels as qualitative internal judgments without numerical acceptance thresholds. Removed unqualified support wording from the abstract and index.
- Displayed all ten distinct archived Spearman pairs and described their shared-path/mechanical relationships. No statistical independence, null test or uncertainty interval is implied.
- Distinguished the full H4 census counts of 34/30 from separately ranked tail views.
- Limited holdout terminology to the C55 branch; a complete cross-project access/use history was not available.
- Rounded the displayed drawdown guardrail to approximately -10.54R and identified its source as the previous frozen four-pack's observed MaxDD. Archived full-precision calculations are unchanged.
- Documented the two sequential 252-observation windows and the earliest possible Pulse at row 545 under complete inputs, explaining the archived 99/643 and 103/647 availability counts.
- Published the fixed display cuts, bit weights and the fact that their original calibration rationale was not recovered.
- Identified missing configuration/build metadata in old exports and the absence of matched event base rates. No historical inputs or provenance were fabricated.
- Added canonical handoff paths and a combined edition map. The reader and tests are already in the repository; preserve its folders when downloading.

### Not performed or still unknown

- No new market-data run, strategy optimization, null/baseline experiment, confidence interval or event-base-rate study.
- No new numerical criteria were invented to make the historical support judgment pass retrospectively.
- Cross-project pristine-holdout status, original cut-calibration provenance and historical exporter configuration remain unverified.
- The private exporter was not modified to emit configuration/build metadata. A future change would be a separate engineering task.
- The previously renamed 00–04 handoff files were not available for inspection. This release supplies a canonical repository handoff; it does not claim those old files were repaired.
- Full private market/audit datasets were not redistributed. Public code availability does not make every archived empirical count publicly reproducible.
