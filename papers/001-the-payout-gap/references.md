# Paper 001 — Bibliography and scope of related work

**Citation check date:** 2026-10-11.

These references are included to **situate**, not to assert priority for, the six-clock measurement synthesis in Paper 001. This is a **selected background bibliography**, not a systematic literature review.

1. **Backtest selection bias:** Bailey, D. H., Borwein, J. M., López de Prado, M., & Zhu, Q. J. (2017). *The probability of backtest overfitting*. *Journal of Computational Finance*. DOI: [10.21314/JCF.2016.322](https://doi.org/10.21314/JCF.2016.322). Describes risk that historical strategy selection inflates apparent performance; does not test this paper's prop-firm cohorts.

2. **Drawdown measurement:** Goldberg, L. R., & Mahmoud, O. (2017). *Drawdown: From practice to theory and back again*. *Mathematics and Financial Economics, 11*, 275–297. DOI: [10.1007/s11579-016-0181-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11579-016-0181-9). Formalizes conditional expected drawdown, a measure of equity-path risk rather than realized external payouts.

3. **Drawdown-constrained portfolio theory:** Maier-Paape, S., & Zhu, Q. J. (2018). *A general framework for portfolio theory. Part II: Drawdown risk measures*. *Risks, 6*(3), 76. DOI: [10.3390/risks6030076](https://doi.org/10.3390/risks6030076). Provides related risk-measure theory; does not imply that ordinary performance statistics universally assume unconstrained capital.

4. **Incomplete event-time observations:** Kaplan, E. L., & Meier, P. (1958). *Nonparametric estimation from incomplete observations*. *Journal of the American Statistical Association, 53*(282), 457–481. DOI: [10.1080/01621459.1958.10501452](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1958.10501452). Motivates careful treatment of incomplete follow-up and censoring. Paper 001 does **not** implement or validate a Kaplan–Meier estimator.

**Interpretation boundary:** None of the citations establishes that Paper 001's six-clock classification is novel worldwide, or that the case program estimates any universal trader payout rate. See [full text](working-paper-v1.0.md) for exact claims and evidence limitations.
