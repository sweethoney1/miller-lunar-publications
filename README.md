**Paper 001 in one sentence:** Backtest return, equity recovery, modeled account-rule survival, payout claims, simulated receipts and actual household cash-flow are different measurable objects; choosing a metric requires specifying the underlying event and timing clock.

### Important reading notes

- **Working paper, not peer-reviewed.** It should not be read as an academic journal acceptance, audited trading performance or investment advice.
- Its quantitative cases come from **one historical research program** with related experiments and differing assumptions. They are not independent cross-market replications.
- Selected historic payout exports were **arithmetically reconciled** against their own summaries, but no full source-code/market-data replay or real prop-firm rule certification is claimed.
- No personal evaluation-purchase-to-real-bank-receipt-to-net-break-even dataset is provided.
- The paper text describes methodology and boundaries; **trading software, private Drive links, account identifiers, and raw licensed datasets are excluded**.
